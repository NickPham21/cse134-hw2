Track and Solve

Track and Solve is a multiplayer game where players compete in a track and field style competition to solve math equations the fastest. It was created with the intention of being educational, primarily for students between elementary and middle school. It was originally developed as a team project for a Software Development course.

Technologies used: Typescript, Socket.io, Node.js
Developers: Nick Pham, Kevin Pantoja, Gram Koski, Munachi Okoro, Melaina Wang
